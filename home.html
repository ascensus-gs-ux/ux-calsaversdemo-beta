Program Benefits

Discover the Value of CalSavers

Whether you’re a Saver or Employer, CalSavers makes retirement savings more accessible through a simple, flexible program supported by educational resources, tools, and ongoing assistance.

Accessible

CalSavers provides an easy way to participate in retirement savings, whether through an employer or independently.

Flexible

Savers can adjust their deduction choices any time, while employers can facilitate participation with limited ongoing responsibilities.

Guidance & Support

Access educational resources, tools, and support designed to help participants and employers navigate the program with confidence.
